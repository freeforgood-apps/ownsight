# Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first, and how it was tested.

## 0.2.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- The thinking loop (stage 2). Still an early test version, not shared yet.
- **Catch a thought in two taps:** write it, tap where it sits in the loop (each stage has a short hint), then tap a topic. That second tap saves it, and **Undo** puts the words back in the box if a tap went wrong.
- **Recent** shows your thoughts, newest first, 20 at a time. Tap one to open it: **Move forward** takes it to its next stage with a question to answer ("How and when will you do it?"), **Edit** fixes the words, stage or topic, and **Delete** removes it. Each has Undo.
- **Patterns** after 5 thoughts: where your thinking leans (with the numbers), the stuck point (like "4 at Idea for every 1 at Plan"), which way you're heading (after 10), a map of the loop with your trails, and which topics are on your mind. Before 5, it shows how many so far and a clearly marked made-up example.
- **Your topics** can be renamed on the More tab. Thoughts keep their topic.
- **Saving on this device:** thoughts save automatically and every save is read back to check it. The top of the screen says "Saved on this device", or explains when it isn't saving. A second open window picks up new thoughts, and a window that's out of date can't save over newer ones. Saved data that can't be read is set aside, never saved over, and you can start fresh with two taps. Data saved by a newer version is shown but left alone.
- Limits, each with a friendly message: 1,000 characters a thought, 10,000 thoughts, 20 characters a topic name.
- Tested: 19 logic tests passed (`node --test`) on the lean, stuck point, heading, topics, trails, limits and reading saved data, with the counts also checked a second way and 10,000 thoughts worked through in under a second. 107 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: the three Done when steps (catch in two taps and see it saved; after 5 thoughts Patterns says which way you lean with the numbers; close and reopen with the thoughts still there), Undo, Move forward, trails, Edit, Delete, topic names, Esc and focus going back, typed HTML and scripts staying plain text, two windows, damaged and newer data, a device that can't save, 0.1.0's settings, keyboard only, offline, every saved name starting with `ownsight-`, and nothing sideways or hidden at 320 × 568 with extra large text (light and dark), 390 × 844, 768 × 1024 with large text and 1280 × 800 dark. 8 of 8 planted bugs were caught (heading missing exactly half a stage, the stuck point ignoring its twice-as-many rule, an older version trimming later fields, trails in the wrong order, too-long text slipping through, a `__proto__` key getting through, an out-of-date window saving over newer thoughts, and damaged data not held back). The accessibility checker found nothing on all 5 tabs, the full Patterns tab, the thought sheet, Move forward, Edit, the trail sheet, the Undo bar, the warning card and the Catch error, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast was computed for 42 color pairs in both themes, plus higher contrast: all pass (text 4.81:1 or more; edges and bars 3.27:1 or more). Security release check: nothing personal or secret, no debug code, no `innerHTML`, no outside requests, and the privacy lock fresh and working. Not yet: VoiceOver on a real iPhone, and the owner's check on a real iPhone on the live site.

## 0.1.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- First version, for testing the frame of the app (stage 1). Not shared yet.
- Installs to the Home Screen with its own icon, opens full screen, works offline after the first visit, and follows light and dark mode. It's built to fit around the notch and the Home bar.
- Five tabs at the bottom, in thumb reach: Catch, Patterns, Decide, Me and More. The first four say what's coming.
- More has a text size choice (normal, large, extra large), steps for adding Ownsight to your Home Screen, and About with the version, the privacy promise and the feedback address.
- A card on Catch suggests adding Ownsight to your Home Screen, with an Install button where the browser offers one. **Got it** hides it for good.
- A privacy lock built into the page blocks it from sending anything over the internet or running any script but its own.
- Tested: 49 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: the page loads with no errors, the privacy lock blocks an outside request, an outside image and an added script, the tabs work by tap and by arrow keys, text size and **Got it** are kept after a reload (a damaged saved size falls back to normal), every saved name starts with `ownsight-v1-`, it opens offline with the version shown, an update clears only Ownsight's old offline copy (another app's stays), and nothing scrolls sideways or hides behind the tab bar at phone size, small phone size with extra large text, and desktop size, in light and dark. 4 of 4 planted bugs were caught (an offline helper that clears other apps' copies, a setting saved without the app's prefix, a script edit with a stale privacy lock, and content hidden under the tab bar). The accessibility checker found nothing on all 5 tabs at 4 sizes in light and dark. Text contrast was computed for every color pair in both themes: all 4.5:1 or more. On a real iPhone (Nov 14): added to the Home Screen from Safari, it opened full screen with its own icon, nothing hid under the notch or the Home bar, and in Airplane Mode it still opened with the version shown.
