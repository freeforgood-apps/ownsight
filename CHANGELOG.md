# Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first, and how it was tested.

## 1.0.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- **The first full release.** About no longer says "early test version".
- **Lock with a PIN** (More tab, optional, off until you turn it on): Ownsight asks for your PIN when it opens, and again after a minute away, so someone else using your phone doesn't see your thoughts. The PIN screen covers everything, an open thought too. Leaving Ownsight covers the screen at once, so the app switcher shows the PIN screen. A wrong PIN never changes anything. After 5 wrong tries in a row it waits 30 seconds, then longer, up to 5 minutes. Only a scrambled form of your PIN is kept on your device, never the PIN itself.
- **The PIN hides your thoughts, but it doesn't cover backups.** A short tip on the PIN card and the backup card says so: to keep a backup private too, protect it with a password.
- **Forgot your PIN?** On the PIN screen, Erase and start over (two taps) deletes everything Ownsight saved on this device, then takes you to Restore a backup.
- **The self-test** (More → About → Run the self-test): it taps through Ownsight in your browser on its own separate storage, checks the counts, dates and reminder timing a second way, tries backups, the automatic copies, the PIN lock, the privacy lock and the offline copy, and shows a report you can copy or email. Your real thoughts are never touched.
- **Delete everything** now removes the PIN lock too.
- Thoughts saved by 0.2.0 through 0.5.0 carry over. Saved data is still version 3.
- Tested: the self-test passed at 3 sizes (phone in light, small phone in dark, desktop), 368 checks each, and again in 13 time zones from UTC−11 to UTC+14 (half-hour and 45-minute zones and midnight clock changes included) and on a made-up Monday, Thursday and Friday. It covers both Done when lines for 1.0 (with the PIN on, Ownsight asks for it before any thought shows, a wrong PIN changes nothing, the right one opens it; and the self-test itself) and both 0.5.0 lines (copy a backup code, delete everything, paste it back, and every thought returns exactly; delete a thought, wait for Undo to go, and two taps bring back this morning's copy). It also runs every main flow; See's headline and count for all 343 mixes of up to 6 thoughts per stage and the reminder timing in 550 cases, each worked out a second way; 4,386 times across every day of 2024 and 2026 against the browser's own calendar; Your week; the first, due and overdue reminders and Later; backup files and codes, with and without a password (wrong, short and right passwords, 600,000 rounds, no readable words); the clipboard not working; backups from 0.2.0 to 0.4.0; wrong-app, newer, damaged, junk and booby-trapped backups; thoughts saved by 0.2.0 to 0.4.0; damaged and newer saved data; two windows; the PIN lock (setting it, the lock covering everything, wrong tries, the wait, a minute away, turning it off, Forgot your PIN? and a backup bringing everything back); text size; the privacy lock; the offline copy; About; and Delete everything leaving the real data untouched. 16 of 16 planted bugs were caught (a wrong PIN opening it, the PIN kept in plain text, the PIN screen not covering everything, no wait after wrong tries, no new lock after a minute away, Erase keeping the PIN, the first reminder a change late, a tie on See read wrong, days counted in UTC, a copy counting as a backup, restore keeping unknown fields, thoughts moving backward, the privacy lock letting requests out, the week a day too long, Delete everything without its second tap, and restore skipping its second tap). Separately in headless Chrome: 22 PIN checks on the real app (locked before any thought shows when it opens, Esc can't close it, a minute away with a thought open behind it, Erase), and 7 more (0.5.0 thoughts opening in 1.0, a PIN turned on or off in one window locking or opening another, typing the PIN and pressing Enter, Tab staying inside the PIN screen, and opening offline with the version shown after visiting the self-test). The accessibility checker found nothing in 29 runs covering every screen on the 0.5.0 list, plus the PIN screen, Forgot your PIN?, Erase waiting for its second tap, the PIN card off and on (with the backup tip), both PIN errors, and the self-test page, at 4 sizes in light and dark; axe found nothing on the self-test's report at 3 sizes. No new colors. Security release check: nothing personal or secret, no debug code, no `innerHTML` (the self-test builds its panel from text too), the PIN hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 rounds, a random salt) and never stored or left on screen, the self-test on its own storage names, only Ownsight's own storage ever cleared, every REVIEW lead looked at (the clipboard only on a tap, the self-test's test request that the privacy lock blocks, the windows' messages), and the privacy lock fresh and working. Two small fixes from that check: the PIN box is cleared after unlocking, and the self-test link opens with `noreferrer`. Not yet: the owner's check on a real iPhone (the 1.0 and 0.5.0 Done when lines), the self-test on a real iPhone, VoiceOver, and the share sheet and clipboard in Safari.

## 0.5.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- **Back up your thoughts** (top of the More tab): save a backup file (through the share sheet, so it can go to Files, iCloud Drive or email) or copy a backup code to paste in Notes. Every backup is checked to restore before it's handed out.
- **An optional password** for a backup: nobody can open it without the password, and that includes you, since a forgotten password can't be recovered. It's real encryption, done on your device.
- **Restore a backup** from a file or a pasted code. When it would replace thoughts that are here, the first tap says what's in it ("This backup has 23 thoughts…") and a second tap replaces them, with **Undo**.
- **Automatic copies:** Ownsight keeps a copy of your thoughts from each of the last 3 days you opened it, on this device. If you delete something by mistake, two taps bring a copy back, with Undo. They aren't a backup, since they're only on this device.
- **Gentle reminders,** timed like Till Payday's: a card on Write after 5 changes with no backup, again after 7 days and 10 changes (or 30 changes), and after 30 days. **Later** waits a few days (a day when it's been 30). Beside the name, "No backup yet" or "Backed up 3 days ago" opens the backups.
- **If saved thoughts can't be read,** the note on Write now offers **Bring back a copy** (an automatic copy or a backup), as well as Start fresh.
- **Delete everything from this device** (bottom of More, two taps): removes every thought, automatic copy and setting Ownsight saved here, and nothing from any other app.
- **A fuller About:** what stays where, how to keep your thoughts safe, and a link to the public code.
- Thoughts saved by 0.2.0, 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 carry over. Saved data is now version 3, which adds when you last backed up. An older Ownsight left open in another window won't save over it.
- Tested: 17 logic tests passed (`node --test`) in 12 time zones, from UTC−11 to UTC+14: moving up version 1 and 2 data, the backup record (kept, or started over when it doesn't check out, never hiding any thoughts), days across month ends, leap days, the year end and every day of a year at 6 times of day (daylight saving included), the reminder timing checked a second way against Till Payday's rules in over 800 cases, backup codes with emoji and line breaks, wrong-app, newer, damaged and `__proto__` backups, known fields only, and keeping 3 copies. 167 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: both Done when steps (copy a backup code, delete everything, paste it back, and every thought returns exactly; delete a thought, wait for Undo to go, and two taps on this morning's copy bring it back), backup files (downloaded, named, chosen again and restored), passwords (too short, wrong, right, a fresh salt and IV each time, no readable words in a protected code, 600,000 PBKDF2 rounds), the second tap timing out and resetting, Undo after a restore, copies made on opening and before the first change of a day when Ownsight stayed open overnight (over 6 made-up days, keeping the newest 3), every reminder and Later, damaged data (bring back a copy or a backup, or start fresh, with what couldn't be read still set aside), newer data left alone, booby-trapped backups and file names staying plain text, two windows, the clipboard being blocked, 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 data, the main flows from 0.4.0, and offline. 12 of 12 planted bugs were caught (keeping 4 copies, the reminder a change late, restore skipping its second tap, Delete everything clearing another app's settings, short passwords, old data starting at 0 changes, changes not counted, a copy counting as a backup, no copy before the day's first change, restore keeping unknown fields, a file name shown as markup, and Later counting as a change). The accessibility checker found nothing on Write with each reminder and the damaged note, More (fresh, with thoughts and copies, and with damaged data), the password box, the backup code box, each error, the restore note and password box, a copy and Delete everything waiting for a second tap, plus every screen on the 0.4.0 list, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast was computed in code for the new colors in both themes: all pass (text 5.37:1 or more, edges 3.70:1 or more). Security release check: nothing personal or secret, no debug code, no `innerHTML`, no outside requests, clipboard only on a tap, only Ownsight's own storage cleared, and the privacy lock fresh and working. Not yet: the owner's check on a real iPhone, VoiceOver, and the share sheet and clipboard in Safari (headless Chrome can't show them).

## 0.4.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- **The remake:** Ownsight is now simpler to understand and use. Still an early test version, not shared yet.
- **Save a thought in one tap:** write it, then tap where it's at: **Thinking about it**, **Planning it** or **Did it**. Each has a short hint. **Undo** puts the words back in the box.
- **Lifts me or weighs on me:** right after saving, a card asks whether it lifts you or weighs on you. It's optional, it stays about 20 seconds, and you can set or change it later from the thought.
- **Move a thought along:** open it and tap its next step. At Thinking about it: **Make a plan** ("What will you do about it?") or **I already did it**. At Planning it: **I did it** ("How did it go?"). Each asks for an optional line, which shows when you open the thought again. **Edit** fixes the words, and **Delete** removes it, each with Undo.
- **See** (was Patterns), from 5 thoughts: a headline from where most of your thoughts sit ("You do a lot of thinking."), the count ("8 of your 12 thoughts are still at Thinking about it."), a bar in three parts with the numbers beside it, what lifts you and what weighs on you (with the newest 3 of each), and how many thoughts are waiting for a plan, with **Show me one**.
- **Your week:** from Friday to Sunday, See starts with a look back at your last 7 days: how many thoughts you wrote and did, what lifted you, what weighed on you, and "What would you like more of next week?"
- **Three tabs:** Write, See and More. Topics, the six-stage loop, trails, the Map and the Decide and Me tabs are gone, to keep it straightforward.
- **Your thoughts carry over** from 0.2.0 and 0.3.0: Notice, Question and Idea become Thinking about it, Plan becomes Planning it, and Do and Check become Did it. Topics and links between thoughts go. They're saved in the new way with your next change, and an older Ownsight left open in another window won't save over them.
- Tested: 19 logic tests passed (`node --test`) in 12 time zones, from UTC−11 to UTC+14, including Chile's midnight clock changes: moving up real data saved through the 0.2.0 and 0.3.0 screens, reading saved data (damaged, newer, extra fields, `__proto__`), the See headline and counts (also counted a second way), lifts and weighs, waiting for a plan, moving along, and Your week at the Friday and Sunday edges, across month ends, a leap day, the year end and daylight saving changes, plus 3,000 made-up thoughts counted a second way at 120 made-up times. 191 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: the two Done when steps (save in one tap, open it, Make a plan and it moves to Planning it; See shows where thoughts sit and what lifts or weighs at a glance), the card after saving and its question, Undo for every change, Your week in 3 time zones at Thursday night, Friday just after midnight, Sunday night and Monday, 0.3.0 data moving up with a 0.3.0 window open beside it, two windows, damaged and newer data, keyboard only, offline, the 10,000 limit, saved names, and nothing sideways or covered at 320 × 568 with extra large text in dark, 390 × 844 and 1280 × 800. 11 of 11 planted bugs were caught (an old stage landing in the wrong place, the week one day too long, Sunday left out, reading trimming later fields, moving backward, a tie read wrong, Planning counted as done, a `__proto__` key getting through, an out-of-date window saving over newer thoughts, the card after saving leaving after 10 seconds, and lifts not being saved). The accessibility checker found nothing on all 3 tabs, See with Your week, the thought sheet at each stage, Make a plan, Edit, the Undo bar, the card after saving (with a choice pressed), the Write error and the warning card, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast was computed in code for the new colors in both themes: all pass (text 7.10:1 or more, the pressed ring 5.37:1 or more, and the edges of the bar and its key 3.70:1 or more; the bar's counts are also in words beside it). Security release check: nothing personal or secret, no debug code, no `innerHTML`, no outside requests, and the privacy lock fresh and working. On a real iPhone (Nov 14), on the live site: the owner's quick check passed (saving a thought in one tap, opening it and tapping Make a plan moved it to Planning it, and See showed where thoughts sit and what lifts or weighs at a glance). Not yet: VoiceOver.

## 0.3.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- **Show me one:** when Patterns finds a stuck point, a button under it opens the oldest thought still waiting at that stage (one you haven't moved forward yet), ready to move forward. Tap it again after moving one forward, and the next one opens.
- **Pick a topic above Recent** to see only that topic's thoughts. **All** shows everything again. Catching a thought in another topic switches back to All, so you always see what you just saved. Ownsight always opens showing All.
- Fixed: after deleting a thought, focus could land on a heading instead of going back where you were.
- Tested: 20 logic tests passed (`node --test`), including the new pick for Show me one (oldest still waiting, then the oldest there if all have moved forward). 124 browser checks passed in headless Chrome, twice in a row: everything from 0.2.0 plus the two new Done when steps, focus going back to Show me one after moving forward or deleting, Undo from there, the filter read out, focus staying on the filter, an empty topic, catching in another topic or the same one, reopening on All, and the filter fitting at 320 × 568 with extra large text in dark. 2 of 2 new planted bugs were caught (Show me one ignoring thoughts already moved forward, and the filter not filtering). The accessibility checker found nothing on Catch with the filter, a picked filter, Patterns with Show me one and the sheet it opens, plus a fresh start, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Security scan of the changes: nothing personal, no debug code, no `innerHTML`, no outside requests. Not yet: the owner's check on a real iPhone, and VoiceOver.

## 0.2.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- The thinking loop (stage 2). Still an early test version, not shared yet.
- **Catch a thought in two taps:** write it, tap where it sits in the loop (each stage has a short hint), then tap a topic. That second tap saves it, and **Undo** puts the words back in the box if a tap went wrong.
- **Recent** shows your thoughts, newest first, 20 at a time. Tap one to open it: **Move forward** takes it to its next stage with a question to answer ("How and when will you do it?"), **Edit** fixes the words, stage or topic, and **Delete** removes it. Each has Undo.
- **Patterns** after 5 thoughts: where your thinking leans (with the numbers), the stuck point (like "4 at Idea for every 1 at Plan"), which way you're heading (after 10), a map of the loop with your trails, and which topics are on your mind. Before 5, it shows how many so far and a clearly marked made-up example.
- **Your topics** can be renamed on the More tab. Thoughts keep their topic.
- **Saving on this device:** thoughts save automatically and every save is read back to check it. The top of the screen says "Saved on this device", or explains when it isn't saving. A second open window picks up new thoughts, and a window that's out of date can't save over newer ones. Saved data that can't be read is set aside, never saved over, and you can start fresh with two taps. Data saved by a newer version is shown but left alone.
- Limits, each with a friendly message: 1,000 characters a thought, 10,000 thoughts, 20 characters a topic name.
- Tested: 19 logic tests passed (`node --test`) on the lean, stuck point, heading, topics, trails, limits and reading saved data, with the counts also checked a second way and 10,000 thoughts worked through in under a second. 107 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: the three Done when steps (catch in two taps and see it saved; after 5 thoughts Patterns says which way you lean with the numbers; close and reopen with the thoughts still there), Undo, Move forward, trails, Edit, Delete, topic names, Esc and focus going back, typed HTML and scripts staying plain text, two windows, damaged and newer data, a device that can't save, 0.1.0's settings, keyboard only, offline, every saved name starting with `ownsight-`, and nothing sideways or hidden at 320 × 568 with extra large text (light and dark), 390 × 844, 768 × 1024 with large text and 1280 × 800 dark. 8 of 8 planted bugs were caught (heading missing exactly half a stage, the stuck point ignoring its twice-as-many rule, an older version trimming later fields, trails in the wrong order, too-long text slipping through, a `__proto__` key getting through, an out-of-date window saving over newer thoughts, and damaged data not held back). The accessibility checker found nothing on all 5 tabs, the full Patterns tab, the thought sheet, Move forward, Edit, the trail sheet, the Undo bar, the warning card and the Catch error, at 4 sizes in light and dark. Contrast was computed for 42 color pairs in both themes, plus higher contrast: all pass (text 4.81:1 or more; edges and bars 3.27:1 or more). Security release check: nothing personal or secret, no debug code, no `innerHTML`, no outside requests, and the privacy lock fresh and working. Not yet: VoiceOver on a real iPhone, and the owner's check on a real iPhone on the live site.

## 0.1.0 (Nov 14, 2026)
- First version, for testing the frame of the app (stage 1). Not shared yet.
- Installs to the Home Screen with its own icon, opens full screen, works offline after the first visit, and follows light and dark mode. It's built to fit around the notch and the Home bar.
- Five tabs at the bottom, in thumb reach: Catch, Patterns, Decide, Me and More. The first four say what's coming.
- More has a text size choice (normal, large, extra large), steps for adding Ownsight to your Home Screen, and About with the version, the privacy promise and the feedback address.
- A card on Catch suggests adding Ownsight to your Home Screen, with an Install button where the browser offers one. **Got it** hides it for good.
- A privacy lock built into the page blocks it from sending anything over the internet or running any script but its own.
- Tested: 49 browser checks passed in headless Chrome: the page loads with no errors, the privacy lock blocks an outside request, an outside image and an added script, the tabs work by tap and by arrow keys, text size and **Got it** are kept after a reload (a damaged saved size falls back to normal), every saved name starts with `ownsight-v1-`, it opens offline with the version shown, an update clears only Ownsight's old offline copy (another app's stays), and nothing scrolls sideways or hides behind the tab bar at phone size, small phone size with extra large text, and desktop size, in light and dark. 4 of 4 planted bugs were caught (an offline helper that clears other apps' copies, a setting saved without the app's prefix, a script edit with a stale privacy lock, and content hidden under the tab bar). The accessibility checker found nothing on all 5 tabs at 4 sizes in light and dark. Text contrast was computed for every color pair in both themes: all 4.5:1 or more. On a real iPhone (Nov 14): added to the Home Screen from Safari, it opened full screen with its own icon, nothing hid under the notch or the Home bar, and in Airplane Mode it still opened with the version shown.
